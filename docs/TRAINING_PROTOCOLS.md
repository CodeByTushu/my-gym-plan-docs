# Athletic Training Protocols & Hypertrophy Science

This document explains the exercise physiology, load periodization, and recovery logic governing the **7-12-15 Scientific Protocol** and the **2-Tier Room Fitness System**.

## 1. The 7-12-15 Hypertrophy Protocol

Most gym lifters plateau because they focus exclusively on one rep range (e.g., 3 sets of 10 reps). The 7-12-15 protocol deliberately activates all muscle fiber recruitment pathways across mechanical tension, muscle damage, and metabolic stress in each exercise.

### Phase 1: Power Set (7 Reps)
- **Target Load:** 80% to 85% of 1-Rep Max (1RM).
- **Physiology:** Maximizes mechanical tension. Forces the central nervous system (CNS) to fire high-threshold Type IIx fast-twitch motor units (Henneman's Size Principle).
- **Rest Interval:** 90–120 seconds.

### Phase 2: Hypertrophy Growth Set (12 Reps)
- **Target Load:** 65% to 70% of 1RM.
- **Physiology:** Stimulates sarcoplasmic hypertrophy and muscle fiber microtrauma. Prolongs time-under-tension (TUT) to approximately 40–50 seconds per set.
- **Rest Interval:** 60–90 seconds.

### Phase 3: Metabolic Burnout Drop Set (15 Reps)
- **Target Load:** 50% of 1RM (immediate 20–30% drop from Set 2).
- **Physiology:** Drives metabolic accumulation (lactate, inorganic phosphate, hydrogen ions). Induces severe cell swelling and capillary hyperemia (the muscle pump).
- **Rest Interval:** 60 seconds before moving to the next exercise.

## 3. 2-Tier Room Fitness & Calisthenics Engine

### Tier 1: Daily Essentials (Non-Negotiable Habit Anchors)
Executed 7 days a week upon waking to activate synovial fluid, prime posture, and sustain baseline metabolic rate:
1. **10 Push-ups** (Standard or Incline)
2. **10 Hindu Baithak** (Full-depth bodyweight squats with rhythmic arm swings)
3. **60-Second Core Plank** (Strict hollow body alignment)
4. **Doorframe Rows** (Scapular retraction and postural resetting)

### Tier 2: Weekly Progressive Calisthenics Split
For athletes training bodyweight strength:
- **Upper Body Day (Push/Pull):** Pike push-ups, diamond push-ups, pull-ups, chair dips.
- **Hollow Body Core Day:** L-sit progressions, hollow body rocks, mountain climbers.
- **Lower Body Power Day:** Bulgarian split squats, jump squats, single-leg calf raises.

### 48-Hour Physiological Recovery Guard
When an athlete completes a Tier 2 module (e.g., Upper Body Push), the engine stamps the current timestamp into `tushu_room_fitness_v1`. For the subsequent 48 hours:
- Repeat high-intensity loading on that muscle group is flagged as **Resting / Recovering**.
- Active recovery mobility drills and restorative stretching routines are recommended instead.
